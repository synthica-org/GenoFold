Runs locally through all six GenoFold stages: Preprocess, Represent, Infer, Reconstruct, Score, and Explain.

01 / 06 - Preprocess

Filter, correct, align, normalize, and denoise raw NGS and Hi-C data.

02 / 06 - Represent

Convert genomic interactions into graphs and generate sequence embeddings.

03 / 06 - Infer

Apply GNN encoders and transformer attention to capture relationships and predict early 3D representations.

04 / 06 - Reconstruct

Decode learned features into 3D coordinates, spatial distances, and structural domains.

05 / 06 - Score

Estimate prediction reliability and uncertainty on a per-region basis.

06 / 06 - Explain

Generate heatmaps and other summaries highlighting influential loci and structural importance
